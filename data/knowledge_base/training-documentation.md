# Methodology: training and documentation

> Synthetic data.

## Training
- Administrator training (about 3 hours) for the communications or IT team: users and permissions, workflows, navigation, modules and site settings.
- Content editor training (about 2 hours per group) for department editors: creating and updating pages, images, documents, calendar events, forms and accessibility basics.
- Sessions are delivered on site or by video conference, and recorded so new staff can watch them later.
- Training is not limited by number of attendees, and refresher sessions are available at any time during the contract at no extra cost.

## Documentation
- A written user guide for editors and a separate administrator guide, both updated with each product release.
- An online help center with short how-to articles and videos, available from inside the CMS.
- A site-specific handbook delivered at launch: content guidelines, style rules and the list of integrations.
