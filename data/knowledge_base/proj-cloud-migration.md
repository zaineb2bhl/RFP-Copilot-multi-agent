# Past project: Cloud migration for Harbourline Insurance

> Synthetic data. Harbourline Insurance is a fictional client.

## Context
Harbourline Insurance needed to exit its on-premises data centre within 18 months.

## Solution
Between 2022 and 2024 we migrated 41 applications to AWS using a wave-based approach: assessment, landing zone with infrastructure as code (Terraform), migration waves, and optimisation. We set up CI/CD pipelines, centralised logging and monitoring, and automated backups with tested restore procedures. Disaster recovery was designed with a recovery point objective (RPO) of 15 minutes and a recovery time objective (RTO) of 4 hours, validated in two DR exercises.

## Results
- Data centre exit completed 2 months ahead of schedule.
- Infrastructure costs reduced by 27% in the first year.
- Managed hosting and 24/7 support provided by Northbridge Digital since go-live.
