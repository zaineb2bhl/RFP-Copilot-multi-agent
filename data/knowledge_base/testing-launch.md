# Methodology: testing and launch

> Synthetic data.

## Testing
- Functional testing of every template, module and form.
- Cross-browser and device testing on the current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, and on iOS and Android phones and tablets.
- Accessibility testing (automated and manual) with a written report.
- Performance testing: pages load in under 3 seconds on a typical 4G mobile connection for the tested key pages.
- User acceptance testing by city staff on a staging site, with a shared list of issues tracked to resolution.

## Launch
- A launch checklist reviewed with the city: redirects, DNS, analytics, forms routing, search index, SSL.
- Launch is scheduled at a low-traffic time agreed with the city, with no downtime for residents.
- The old website is kept available to the city for 30 days after launch for reference.
