# Northbridge CivicCMS: government modules

> Synthetic data.

All modules below are included in the CivicCMS subscription.

## News and alerts
News articles with categories and featured homepage placement. An emergency alert banner can be published site-wide in one step and scheduled to expire.

## Community calendar
A single, site-wide calendar that every department feeds. Events have categories (for example parks, council meetings, library), locations with maps, and recurring patterns. Visitors can filter by category and subscribe through iCal (ICS) and RSS feeds. Departments can display filtered views of the same calendar on their own pages. Optional public event submission with staff approval.

## Agendas and meetings
Meeting pages for council, boards and commissions, with agendas, minutes, packets and video links, organized by body and date.

## Bids and RFPs
A procurement module to post bids, RFPs and RFQs with open and close dates, documents, addenda and award notices. Vendors can subscribe to email notifications for new postings and addenda. Closed bids move to an archive automatically.

## Document center
A searchable library for public documents (ordinances, budgets, reports, forms) with folders, tags and version history. Replacing a document keeps the same link.

## Online forms
A drag-and-drop form builder for service requests, applications and feedback, with file uploads, conditional logic, spam protection and routing of submissions to the right department by email.

## Notifications
Residents can subscribe to email and text message (SMS) notifications for news, alerts, calendar categories, agendas and bids.

## Search, directory and translation
Site-wide search across pages, documents and calendar events; a staff and department directory; and automatic machine translation of the site into more than 100 languages through a language selector.
