# Methodology: Training, knowledge transfer and data migration

> Synthetic data.

## Training
We train administrators and staff with role-based sessions (on site or remote), recorded videos and written user guides. For the Easthaven portal we trained 120 council staff in 9 sessions, with a satisfaction score of 4.6/5.

## Knowledge transfer and exit
Full technical documentation (architecture, runbooks, API specifications) is delivered and kept up to date. At contract end, a documented exit plan ensures transfer of code, data and knowledge to the client or a new supplier.

## Data migration
Data migration follows a repeatable approach: profiling and cleansing, mapping, trial migrations, reconciliation reports and a final cutover rehearsal. Each migration wave is signed off by the client.
