# Methodology: discovery, design and user experience

> Synthetic data.

## Discovery
- Kickoff workshop with the project team, then stakeholder interviews with department heads (typically 8 to 15 interviews).
- Analytics review of the current website (most visited pages, search terms, devices).
- An online survey for residents and, when the city wants it, short usability tests of the current site with 5 to 8 residents.
- Discovery ends with a written report: goals, audiences, top tasks, integration inventory and content strategy.

## Information architecture
We organize the site around what residents want to do (pay, apply, report, find) rather than the city's org chart. Card sorting and tree testing with residents validate the new navigation before design.

## Visual design
- Two distinct homepage design concepts based on the city's brand, followed by up to three rounds of revisions on the selected concept.
- Interior page templates for departments, services, news, events and landing pages.
- Mobile-first, responsive design tested on phones, tablets and desktops.
- Designs are reviewed for accessibility (contrast, focus, text size) before development.

## Homepage
Homepages are designed around the top resident tasks, with prominent search, an "I want to..." quick-links area, news, upcoming events, and an emergency alert area.
