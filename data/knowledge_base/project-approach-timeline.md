# Methodology: project management and timeline

> Synthetic data.

## Phases and typical timeline
A city website project typically takes 16 weeks (12 weeks for small towns, up to 24 weeks for large counties):
1. Discovery and planning: weeks 1 to 3.
2. Information architecture and design: weeks 4 to 8.
3. Development and CMS configuration: weeks 7 to 11.
4. Content migration and staff training: weeks 10 to 14.
5. Testing, accessibility review and launch: weeks 14 to 16.

## Project management
- A dedicated project manager is the single point of contact from kickoff to launch.
- Weekly 30-minute status calls and a written weekly status report (progress, next steps, risks, decisions needed).
- A shared project plan and task board that the city can access at any time.
- Change requests are documented with their impact on cost and schedule and approved by the city before work starts.

## What we ask from the city
A project lead with authority to approve deliverables, timely review of designs and content (we plan five business days per review), and department contacts for content review.
