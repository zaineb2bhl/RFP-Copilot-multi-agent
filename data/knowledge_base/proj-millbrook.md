# Past project: City of Millbrook website with parks and recreation integration

> Synthetic data. The City of Millbrook is a fictional client.

## Context
Millbrook (population 72,000) wanted one website for all departments, with a single events calendar and easy access to parks and recreation registration, utility payments and the city code.

## What we did (2025, 16 weeks)
- Single community calendar fed by parks, library, council and special events, with category filters and iCal subscriptions; departments show filtered views on their pages.
- Integration with the city's parks and recreation registration system: activity catalog embedded on program pages and deep links to registration.
- Links to the online utility payment portal from a "Pay" quick link on every page, and chapter-level links to the city's published code of ordinances.
- Esri ArcGIS maps for parks, trails and road projects embedded on the relevant pages.
- Emergency alert banner and text message (SMS) notifications for residents.

## Results
- Online program registrations increased by 27% in the first season after launch.
- 3,800 residents subscribed to alert notifications in the first year.
- WCAG 2.1 Level AA conformance, tested by our accessibility specialist and verified with an external audit.
