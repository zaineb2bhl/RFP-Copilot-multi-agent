# Methodology: Support, maintenance and service levels

## Service model
After go-live, our ITIL-aligned service desk provides application support and maintenance. Standard support hours are 08:00-18:00 on business days, with optional 24/7 on-call cover for priority 1 incidents.

## Service levels
- P1 (service down): response within 1 hour, resolution target 4 hours.
- P2 (major degradation): response within 4 hours, resolution target 1 business day.
- P3 (minor issue): response within 1 business day, resolution target 5 business days.

We commit to 99.9% monthly availability for hosted platforms, measured by external monitoring, with service credits if missed.

## Maintenance
Monthly maintenance releases, security patches within 14 days (critical within 48 hours), quarterly service reviews and an annual technology roadmap. A warranty period of 3 months after go-live is included at no extra cost.
