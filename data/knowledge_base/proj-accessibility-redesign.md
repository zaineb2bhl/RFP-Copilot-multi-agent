# Past project: Accessible website redesign for Calder University

> Synthetic data. Calder University is a fictional client.

## Context
Calder University's public website failed an accessibility audit, with more than 300 WCAG issues.

## Solution
In 2024 Northbridge Digital ran user research with 24 participants, including screen-reader users and people with cognitive impairments. We rebuilt the design system with accessible components and integrated automated accessibility testing (axe-core) into the CI pipeline, complemented by manual testing with NVDA, JAWS and VoiceOver.

## Results
- WCAG 2.1 AA conformance confirmed by an external audit, with an accessibility statement published.
- Task completion rate for key journeys increased from 71% to 93%.
- Content editors trained to publish accessible content (6 training sessions, 58 editors).
