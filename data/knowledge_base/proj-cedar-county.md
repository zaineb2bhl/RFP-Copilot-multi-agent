# Past project: Cedar County website and document center

> Synthetic data. Cedar County is a fictional client.

## Context
Cedar County (population 210,000) published more than 12,000 PDF documents across 30 departments, and residents could not find agendas, ordinances or forms.

## What we did (2023, 22 weeks)
- County website redesign on Northbridge CivicCMS with a central document center and per-department sections managed by 85 editors with role-based permissions and approval workflows.
- Integration of the county's agenda management system so that board agendas, minutes and meeting videos appear automatically on meeting pages.
- Bids and RFPs module replacing an email list: vendors subscribe to notifications for new bids and addenda.
- Accessibility review of the 500 most downloaded PDFs, remediated through our remediation partner; other documents were checked on upload in the CMS.

## Results
- WCAG 2.1 Level AA conformance confirmed by the county's external auditor.
- 1,150 vendors subscribed to bid notifications in the first six months.
- Phone calls to the clerk's office about finding documents fell by 40% in the first year, as reported by the county.
