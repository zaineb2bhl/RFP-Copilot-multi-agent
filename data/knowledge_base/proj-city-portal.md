# Past project: Easthaven municipal e-services portal

> Synthetic data. Easthaven Borough Council is a fictional client.

## Context
Easthaven Borough Council (population 210,000) needed to replace 14 paper and email processes with a single online portal for residents: waste collection requests, parking permits, council tax queries and complaints.

## Solution
Between February 2023 and January 2024, Northbridge Digital delivered a responsive citizen portal built on React and a .NET 8 API layer, hosted on Microsoft Azure in the UK South region. Residents sign in with a single account using OpenID Connect, and staff use Azure AD single sign-on. The portal integrates with the council's existing Salesforce CRM and its payment provider through REST APIs.

## Results
- 14 services moved online in 11 months, delivered in 2-week sprints.
- 63% of service requests submitted online after six months (from 12%).
- WCAG 2.1 AA conformance confirmed by an independent accessibility audit.
- 99.95% measured availability during the first year of operation.
