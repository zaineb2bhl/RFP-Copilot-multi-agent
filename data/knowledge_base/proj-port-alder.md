# Past project: Town of Port Alder website

> Synthetic data. The Town of Port Alder is a fictional client.

## Context
Port Alder (population 9,000) had a limited budget, a two-person communications team and an outdated website that staff could not update without a contractor.

## What we did (2024, 12 weeks)
- Quick discovery (5 interviews and an analytics review) and one homepage design concept with two revision rounds, using our pre-built accessible templates.
- Migration of 380 pages and 900 documents with redirects.
- Training on front-end live editing so staff can update pages directly on the site; 9 staff trained in 2 sessions.
- Online forms replacing paper forms for permits, facility rentals and public records requests.

## Results
- Staff now publish all updates themselves; the town ended its web maintenance contractor agreement.
- 14 paper forms moved online in the first year.
- WCAG 2.1 Level AA conformance at launch.
