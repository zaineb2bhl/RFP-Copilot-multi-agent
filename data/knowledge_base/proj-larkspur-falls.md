# Past project: City of Larkspur Falls website redesign

> Synthetic data. The City of Larkspur Falls is a fictional client.

## Context
Larkspur Falls (population 48,000) had a 10-year-old website on an unsupported open-source CMS, with 2,400 pages, inconsistent design and many accessibility errors.

## What we did (2024, 17 weeks)
- Discovery with 12 department interviews, a resident survey (640 responses) and card sorting with residents.
- New information architecture organized around resident tasks; the site was reduced to 1,350 pages.
- Custom design on Northbridge CivicCMS with the community calendar, news, agendas and meetings, bids and RFPs, document center and online forms modules.
- Migration of content and 4,100 documents, with 1,900 permanent (301) redirects.
- Staff single sign-on through Microsoft Entra ID; 46 content editors trained in 5 sessions.

## Results
- Independent accessibility audit before launch: WCAG 2.1 Level AA conformance with no open issues.
- Mobile visits rose from 51% to 64% of traffic in the first year; average time to find a service page fell, measured by a 32% drop in site searches per visit.
- Launched on schedule and on budget.
