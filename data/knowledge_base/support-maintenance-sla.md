# Methodology: support, maintenance and service levels

> Synthetic data.

## Support channels and hours
Support by phone, email and a ticket portal from 8:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. Eastern Time on business days. Emergency support for critical incidents (site down, security issue) is available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

## Response times
- Critical (site down or security incident): response within 1 hour, 24/7.
- High (a major feature not working): response within 4 business hours.
- Normal (questions, minor issues): response within 1 business day.

## Included in the annual subscription
- Hosting, security updates, backups and monitoring.
- Unlimited support requests from trained staff.
- Quarterly CivicCMS feature releases.
- A yearly review meeting with recommendations based on analytics and accessibility scans.

## Warranty and post-launch support
After launch, a 90-day warranty covers the correction of any defect in the work we delivered at no extra cost, and the project team stays assigned for the first 30 days to fix issues and add missing redirects.

## End of contract
At the end of the contract the city can export all content, documents and media in standard formats (HTML, CSV, original files), and we assist with the transition.
