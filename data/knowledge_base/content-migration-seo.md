# Methodology: content migration and SEO

> Synthetic data.

## Content audit
Before migration we crawl the existing website and produce a content inventory: every page and document with its traffic, last update date and owner. With each department we decide what to keep, rewrite, merge or archive. City sites typically remove 30% to 50% of outdated pages during this step.

## Migration
- Automated migration of pages, news, events and documents from the old CMS, followed by manual clean-up of formatting.
- Our content team migrates and formats priority pages; departments review their pages before launch.
- Documents are migrated to the document center with their folder structure.

## Preserving search rankings
- A redirect map links every old URL to its new address with permanent (301) redirects, so bookmarks and search results keep working.
- Page titles, meta descriptions and headings are reviewed for search engines.
- XML sitemap and robots settings are generated automatically, and the new sitemap is submitted to Google Search Console at launch.
- After launch we monitor 404 errors for 30 days and add any missing redirects.
